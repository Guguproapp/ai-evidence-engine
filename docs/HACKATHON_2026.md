## Confirmed

- Online event: August 17–September 3, 2026; in-person and awards: September 2–3 in Santa Clara.
- Submission deadline: September 3, 2026 at 10:00 AM Pacific Time. Late projects are not accepted.
- Age 18+; Taiwan is allowed. Devpost displays exclusions including Brazil, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Quebec, and Russia.
- Teams contain 1–5 people. More than five makes the team prize-ineligible.
- Build a mobile, consumer web, or business web application from scratch.
- A project needs name, elevator pitch, creators/team, and a description of the build story. Images, live URL, and video are recommended globally; individual sponsor challenges can make repository/video/API use mandatory.
- Overall judging: progress, concept/real problem, and feasibility as a startup or company.
- Overall top five are instructed to pitch onsite in Santa Clara on September 3. The instructions state that selected teams are called at 1 PM and must be onsite at 2 PM for a 2:30 PM pitch. Online entry is valid, but an overseas team unable to attend should not assume eligibility for the overall final.
- Sponsors are responsible for delivering sponsor prizes.

## Sponsor fit checkpoint

Nutrient DWS is the strongest natural fit for AI Evidence Engine: its challenge explicitly values deterministic document operations, audit trails, digital signatures, and human review. It requires meaningful DWS use, a public/shared repository with setup, a 2–4 minute working demo, and an explanation of where DWS performs the core operation.

Doctavian also fits document generation/signing but demands a real API call. SerpApi fits evidence discovery but is secondary to provenance. Perfect Corp strongly favors consumer/retail experiences and therefore favors ProofCart AI.
