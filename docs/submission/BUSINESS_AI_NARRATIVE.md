# Business and AI Narrative

Status: **READY**

AI Evidence Engine (AEE) helps creators and small businesses preserve a verifiable evidence history around valuable digital work. Photographers, designers, illustrators, authors, content creators, agencies, brand teams, and AI-assisted creators often keep final files but lose the sequence of source files, edits, approvals, tools, and timestamps that explains how a work evolved. Large enterprises can buy specialized provenance, security, and legal infrastructure. Small creators usually cannot. AEE is designed to make that evidence workflow accessible.

AEE follows two truthful paths. When trusted provenance already exists, it verifies content hashes, digital signatures, C2PA manifests, Registry evidence, and signed parent-child version history. When prior provenance is unavailable, AEE does not guess the past. The creator can explicitly establish a First-Seen evidence point. AEE records when it received, fingerprinted, signed, and sealed that exact version while clearly stating that earlier history remains unknown. Later material changes create new signed child Events instead of overwriting the previous Event, producing a verifiable V1 → V2 → V3 history.

This fits Small Business Services because it gives everyday creative businesses infrastructure that helps them compete, document delivery, review revisions, explain AI involvement, and organize evidence they may later choose to present when protecting their interests. AEE is not a copyright registry, an authorship authority, a fake-image detector, or a court-certified service. It preserves verifiable technical records that may form one part of a broader evidence package.

Humans remain responsible for creating or selecting work, deciding when to start a First-Seen record, approving edits, deciding what may be disclosed, interpreting legal significance, and making business decisions. Deterministic code—not a language model—calculates SHA-256, verifies signatures and C2PA integrity, checks parent chains, assigns the provenance state, compares trusted versions, produces modification masks, and confirms evidence continuity.

Gemini 2.5 Flash runs in the deployed application through Vertex AI. After deterministic verification is complete, the Cloud Run Evidence Explainer receives only allowlisted structured facts and converts them into plain language. Gemini cannot change Integrity, Provenance, Signature, Hash, C2PA, Retention, or Evidence Validity. This gives non-technical creators and customers a useful explanation without allowing an LLM hallucination to become evidence.

Google Cloud supports the live business workflow. Cloud Run hosts the Gemini Evidence Explainer and the Development/Test continuity services. Vertex AI provides the deployed Gemini call. Google Cloud Storage supports a Development/Test Evidence Black Box prototype with short retention, generation preconditions, retrieval, and SHA-256 reverification. Real tests recorded Google rejecting deletion with HTTP 403 while retention was active and rejecting overwrite with HTTP 412. This prototype is clearly separated from the public production verifier and is not represented as permanent preservation.

AI also supported internal development operations during the hackathon: requirements organization, code assistance, test design, documentation, QA planning, and submission preparation. Human review retained control of product scope, evidence rules, security boundaries, deployments, financial statements, legal attestations, and final submission. AEE is therefore AI-assisted in operations and uses Gemini in production, but it is not represented as a fully autonomous company.

The project was built during the hackathon period. The first verified Git commit, GitHub repository creation, Google Cloud project creation, Cloud Run deployments, and production logs all date from August 2026. Pre-existing frameworks, open-source libraries, standard cryptography, C2PA tooling, and Google services are disclosed separately and are not claimed as new AEE inventions.

The proposed business model starts with a free creator entry tier, then adds paid evidence storage, Evidence Package export, professional and agency plans, and usage-based APIs for marketplaces and content platforms. Current external users and revenue are both zero and are disclosed honestly. The product is technically operational, but customer acquisition and paid validation remain the next business step.

Current production capabilities are public provenance verification, known-version modification comparison, C2PA and signature checks, version history, Gemini explanation, First-Seen registration, and persistent V1 → V2 history. The Google Cloud Evidence Black Box remains a Development/Test prototype. Full Soft Binding recovery, invisible watermarking, audio, video, CAD, 3D, manufacturing profiles, and single-asset forensic modification detection are future work, not current claims.
