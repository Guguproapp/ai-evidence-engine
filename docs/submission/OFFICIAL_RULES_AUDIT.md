## Confirmed requirements

- Submission deadline: 2026-08-17 13:00 PDT / 2026-08-18 04:00 GMT+8.
- Individual entrants are permitted. An individual entrant does not need an organization Corporate ID.
- The submitted project/business must have been created after 2026-05-19.
- Reused generic templates, frameworks, boilerplate, or code snippets must be disclosed.
- The product must use at least one Google Cloud product.
- A deployed product with LLM functionality must include at least one deployed Gemini API call.
- Necessary source code must be accessible to judges and carry relevant licensing.
- The demo video must show a functioning product, be shorter than three minutes, be judge-accessible, and use authorized material.
- Revenue, monthly revenue, related-party revenue, expenses, marketing/customer-acquisition spend, users, product evidence, and testing access must be disclosed truthfully.
- English submission materials are required.

## FAQ clarifications

- There is no minimum revenue or customer threshold; zero must be disclosed honestly.
- Vertex AI Gemini satisfies the Gemini requirement.
- No new legal entity is required to enter.
- Repository access may be public or private if judges receive the required access.
