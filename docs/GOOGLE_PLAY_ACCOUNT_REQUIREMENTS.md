# Google Play Account Requirements

Known owner account: `gugupro.app@gmail.com` was identified by the owner as the Google account previously used during an earlier renovation-estimation app effort.

Current Play Console developer-account status and creation date: **UNKNOWN until read directly from Play Console**. The existence of a Google account does not prove that registration fee, identity verification, or developer account activation was completed.

Owner-only checklist:

- Sign in to Play Console with the intended account.
- Confirm Personal or Organization account type.
- Confirm legal name and public developer name.
- Confirm legal address, contact email, contact phone, and developer/support email.
- Confirm Google Payments profile and any registration fee actually paid.
- Complete identity verification if requested.
- Review and accept current Google Play agreements.
- Confirm proposed package before app creation.
- Enable Google Play App Signing.
- Perform any irreversible release or Production submission.

Do not store government documents, address, phone, payment details, credentials, OAuth tokens, or signing keys in this public repository.

If the account is a Personal developer account created after 2023-11-13, Google currently requires a closed test with at least 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days before applying for Production access: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465
