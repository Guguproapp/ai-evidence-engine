# Google SynthID Adapter Status

Status: **interface research only; not integrated**.

Official Google material confirms SynthID for image, text, audio, and video. SynthID Text has an open implementation for model-side watermarking/detection. The general image/video/audio Detector is exposed through Gemini and an early-tester portal; no unrestricted public third-party verification API was confirmed at this checkpoint. No mock detector is provided.
